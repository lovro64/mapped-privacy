Mapped privacy policy

Last updated 23 November 2026

The short version

Mapped works without an account. Your workouts, body data and settings stay on your phone. Only if you turn on Friends (optional) does a small part go online, described below. We do not sell data, show ads or use analytics.

Friends (optional)

If you sign in to Friends with Google, Mapped stores on Google Firebase (servers in the EU): your Google account ID, the username and display name you choose, who you are friends with, people you blocked, the workout summaries you choose to share (workout name, date, minutes, number of sets, total weight lifted and gold PRs) and the fist bumps you give. If you use Diet mode and keep "Compete with friends" on, your friends also see how many days this week you hit your calorie and protein targets and your streak (only those numbers, never what you ate or your weight). Your email address is used only by Google to sign you in and is not shown to anyone. Shared summaries are visible only to your friends at the time you share them. Your full history, body measurements, photos and heart-rate or other health data are never uploaded. Reports you send about a post go to us so we can remove abuse. You can delete your account in the app (Friends > your name > Delete my account): this removes everything listed here from the server. Friends is for people aged 16 and over.

What is stored on your phone

Workouts, sets, routines, notes, bodyweight and measurements, the food you log and your foods, progress photos you add, heart-rate samples recorded during workouts, and your settings. All of it lives in a database inside the app. Deleting the app or using "Reset all data" removes it.

Diet mode and barcode scanning

Diet mode keeps what you eat, your foods and your weigh-ins on your phone only. Built-in foods come with the app. When you scan a barcode, only the barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org, an open food database) to look the product up; when you search branded products online, only the words you typed are sent. Nothing else about you is included. The camera is used only to read the barcode: no picture is taken or saved, and nothing from the camera leaves your phone.

Health Connect and Apple Health

Only if you turn it on, Mapped reads heart rate, resting heart rate, heart-rate variability, sleep, active calories and weight to estimate recovery and workout calories. It can write finished workouts and their calories back. This data is used on your phone only and is never sent anywhere.

Bluetooth heart-rate sensors

If you connect a chest strap or watch over Bluetooth, Mapped reads your heart rate during workouts. The sensor name is saved so it can reconnect next time.

Backups and exports

Backups and CSV exports are files you create and choose where to save (for example Google Drive). Mapped does not upload them anywhere by itself.

Purchases

Mapped Pro is sold through Google Play or the App Store. Purchases are checked with RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app ID and the store receipt, never your workouts or health data. See https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.

Crash reports

If the app crashes, a short error report is saved on your phone. It is only sent if you choose to share it from Settings.

Legal basis

Everything on your phone is processed only on your phone. Friends runs on your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)), and sharing your diet consistency on your explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a)), because it says something about your eating. Health Connect and Bluetooth data are read only after you allow them and never leave your phone. Purchases are handled to fulfil your subscription (Art. 6(1)(b)). You can withdraw consent any time: delete your Friends account, turn off sharing, or revoke permissions in Android settings.

Where your data is stored and for how long

Friends data is stored in Google Firebase Firestore in the EU (multi-region eur3). Google Firebase Authentication and RevenueCat may process sign-in and purchase data outside the EU (for example in the United States) under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses. Friends data is kept until you delete your account (in the app, or by emailing us); reports about posts are kept for up to 12 months. Data on your phone stays until you delete it or the app.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask for a copy of your data, have it corrected or deleted, take it with you (Settings > Back up now exports everything on your phone as a file), object to processing, and withdraw consent. Email us and we answer within one month. You can also complain to your data protection authority; in Croatia that is AZOP (azop.hr). Mapped makes no automated decisions about you with legal or similar effects: calorie and training suggestions are advice you can ignore.

Children

Mapped is not aimed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be in the app and on this page. Questions: mappedapp.support@gmail.com.
